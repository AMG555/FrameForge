# Motion graphics style #104: Storybook 3D

- **ID:** `storybook-3d`
- **Family:** 3D
- **Canvas:** `1920x1080`
- **Fonts:** Fraunces SemiBold (`Title`)
- **Three.js 3D:** Yes (Three.js WebGL Toy World Engine)
- **Footage Overlay:** No

## Look
A whimsical 3D toy-like storybook explainer: bright daylight sky gradient (#7fb6e8 to #cfe6f2 to warm cream #f6e7c8), fresh grass greens and warm sand, rounded toy character anatomy with mitten hands and dot eyes, cel shading with 3 light bands, thin dark outlines (#2a2230), and warm upper-left sun with soft shadows.

## How It Explains
Explains concepts or narrative journeys through tactile, grounded 3D toy models. Works for both stories and step-by-step explainers: machines, biology, physics, architecture, or character tales. Springy arrival settles, drifting cameras, and tactile sound design (marimba, bells, shaker, footstep grass, boings, pops).

THE STYLE

- Typography: Fraunces SemiBold in warm white (#fffaf0) with soft drop shadow, centered high.
- Motion: Eased spring curves, drifting camera moves, secondary motion (grass sway, cloud drift, blinks). Dissolves between scenes, whip pans for energy, cuts on action.
