# Motion graphics style #103: Cinematic Anime

- **ID:** `cinematic-anime`
- **Family:** Illustrated
- **Canvas:** `1920x1080`
- **Fonts:** Noto Serif JP / Fraunces (warm quiet title serif)
- **Three.js 3D:** No (HTML5 2D Canvas Procedural Ink & Watercolor Engine)
- **Footage Overlay:** No

## Look
Classic Japanese hand-drawn feature animation in the tradition of legendary Tokyo studios: tapered ink lines with variable weights, cel shading with designed shadow shapes, painted watercolor/gouache skies and foliage, organic paper tooth, atmospheric distance haze, and living weather.

## How It Explains
Every scene feels like a genuine film moment with emotional resonance. Characters animate on twos (12 fps key poses) while camera drifts smoothly on ones. Rich visual composition with foreground framing, secondary wind physics (grass sway, fluttering clothes, leaves, clouds), and subtle character acting (blinks, breath, weight).

THE STYLE
- Line: Tapered ink lines (fine at ends, fuller in middle, heavier away from light). Warm sepia-black (#2c221e). Inner detail lines thinner than outlines.
- Color & Shading: Cel shading with designed shadow shapes. Lighter, warmer shadows, never black. Characters carry saturated accents; backgrounds are soft and atmospheric.
- Backgrounds: Painted watercolor/gouache. Skies from horizontal brush dabs over wash, foliage as scalloped clumps with leaf marks, lit cumulus clouds, paper texture over plate.
- Motion: Characters on twos (12 drawings/sec) with strong silhouette key poses; cameras drift on ones. Anticipation before moves, squash/weight on landings, secondary wind physics.
- Composition: Film-like framing: wide establishing, medium action, emotional close-up, scenic release. Eye-level, big skies, rule of thirds.
