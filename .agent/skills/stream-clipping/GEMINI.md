# stream-clips

This folder contains the **stream-clips** skill: it turns long livestreams and broadcast recordings (live news channels, debates, press conferences, podcasts, talk shows, sports, gaming) into ready-to-post clips in both portrait (9:16) and landscape (16:9) formats.

Features:
- Dual-orientation support: 9:16 vertical (Shorts/Reels/TikTok) and 16:9 widescreen (YouTube/X/broadcast).
- Flexible duration: from 20-second quick social bites up to 5–10 minute complete news reports and debate segments without cutoff.
- Broad multilingual speech processing with extensive coverage of world and Indian languages (Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Kannada, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati, Punjabi, Urdu, English, Spanish, etc.), supporting native scripts and Romanized transliteration.
- Content-aware moment extraction for news clashes, breaking announcements, interviews, and gaming highlights.

When the user asks to clip a stream, find streams, or extract highlights from a broadcast, read `SKILL.md` in this folder and follow it. In Gemini CLI / Antigravity use `--brain gemini` (or `--brain agent` to pick moments directly).
